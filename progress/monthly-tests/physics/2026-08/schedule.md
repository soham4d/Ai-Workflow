## Cycle Objective

Use the sequence of generated papers as a feedback loop rather than treating each paper as an isolated test:

1. **Paper 1:** Diagnose weaknesses.
2. **Paper 2:** Check whether identified gaps are improving under standard exam conditions.
3. **Paper 3:** Stress-test understanding with harder/trickier problems.
4. **Paper 4:** Directly target remaining weaknesses.
5. **Paper 5:** Full timed mock to measure overall readiness.
6. **16 Aug:** Consolidate the error log, formulas, and concepts without starting major new topics.
7. **17 Aug:** Light recall and sit the monthly test.
